# Zettelkasten

- **Mode:** Operate. The learner studies flashcards in short rounds on an iPhone and in longer sessions on a desktop; scanability and a clear rule outrank expression.
- **Request (user, 2026-11-09):** a Zettelkasten, prominent on the home screen. Question on the front, answer on the back; known → the card moves one compartment forward, not known → back to the very back; repeat until every card is two compartments forward. Own notes can be practised as cards; the box is prefilled from all 31 lessons (concepts, exam traps, takeaways).
- **World:** established (DESIGN.md, Fluent 2 / Microsoft Learn). The surface extends it and changes no tokens.
- **Structure:** derived six candidates (box as stage, study desk, session first, index dividers by module, one column per compartment, daily round); `concept-seed.mjs --scope surface --mode operate` assigned **#4, index dividers by module (Register nach Modul)**. seed: no reference (this concept-seed build prints only a raw key and has no --check-seed). Dealt alternates, not taken: zoo map, timetable slide rack, doujin catalog.
- **Signature:** the three-compartment box with exact counts; the one authored motion is the card flying into its compartment after a rating, counts changing on landing.
- **Decisions after the finish review:** learning paths get their own dividers with a path round (up to 20 cards); resets ask first; the box's rule captions are ink, not link blue; card sides are Vorderseite/Rückseite so vorn/hinten only mean the box.
